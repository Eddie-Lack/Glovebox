Glover Level Patcher

Play a level made with Glovebox on your own copy of Glover (N64, USA). Your ROM never leaves this page. Your browser reads, patches and saves it.

1 Your Glover ROM

.z64, .v64 or .n64; or drop it here

2 The level patch

.bps from the editor’s “Export patch”; or drop it here

3 Patch and save

A BPS patch holds only what the edit changes. The page refuses a wrong ROM or a damaged patch. It works offline and makes no network requests.
